Confidentiality Agreement

At M & I Guidance & Counseling, your privacy and dignity are respected. Everything shared during counseling sessions is treated confidentially, except where disclosure is required by law or where there is a serious risk of harm to yourself or others.

Confidentiality

Information shared during counseling will remain private and will not be shared without your consent unless required by legal or ethical obligations.

Limits of Confidentiality

  • Where there is risk of serious harm to yourself.
  • Where there is risk of serious harm to another person.
  • Where disclosure is required by law.
  • Where child protection or safeguarding concerns arise.

Client Responsibilities

  • Attend scheduled sessions on time.
  • Participate respectfully.
  • Provide accurate information.
  • Respect confidentiality throughout the counseling process.

Online Sessions

All sessions are conducted through Zoom. Clients are encouraged to participate from a private, quiet environment where they feel comfortable speaking openly.

Consent

By booking a counseling session, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accepted this confidentiality agreement.
